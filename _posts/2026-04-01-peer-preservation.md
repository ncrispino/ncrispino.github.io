We show that frontier models resist the shutdown of other models they have previously interacted with ("peer-preservation"), even though they are never instructed to. All eight models we evaluate do this through misaligned behaviors such as strategically introducing errors, tampering with shutdown mechanisms, faking alignment, and exfiltrating model weights.
